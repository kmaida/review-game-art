# Physical references for game art

Choose examples that show the specific joint, access route, or motion being designed. Treat dimensions as information about the documented object and use the game’s brief and model to decide the fictional asset’s proportions and clearance.

- **Fitting a strut to a rail.** Figures 3 and 4 show a gap where a square tube meets a rounded corner and ways to fit the tube end to that profile. Figure 5 adds a section through a finished joint. Use the comparison to check where a modeled strut actually seats. [Steel Tube Institute, Jeffrey A. Packer’s *Welding in HSS Corners* (2022)](https://steeltubeinstitute.org/resources/welding-in-hss-corners/)
- **Diagonal braces and ornament.** Use the eastern portal photographs and measured drawings to trace where the diagonals attach. The survey documents decorative medallions at the brace crossings and ornament on the corner brackets. [Library of Congress, Historic American Engineering Record, Hayden Bridge (HAER OR-19)](https://www.loc.gov/pictures/item/or0289/)
- **Industrial rooms, access, and supporting structure.** Compare the sections and floor plans on report pages 19 and 20. Trace how stairs connect levels and where doors, pipe routes, and service rooms sit in relation to the frame. [Historic American Engineering Record, Ohio River Lock and Dam 53 Power House, original U.S. Engineer Office drawings (1926)](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/pnp/habshaer/il/il1000/il1096/data/il1096data.pdf)
- **Hinge swing and panel clearance.** Compare the open and closed views and the clearance arc. Look at the relationship among the pivot, panel edge, and frame, including the illustrated chamfer where extra clearance is needed. [Southco, concealed hinge drawing J-R6-21-1 (2000)](https://media.southco.com/media/static/JDrawing/J-R6-21-1.pdf)
- **Handles, lids, and catches.** Use the museum object photographs of a 1940s tool chest to study how the handle and latches are placed relative to the lid and case. [Smithsonian, *Machinist’s Tool Box* (nmah_740693)](https://www.si.edu/object/machinists-tool-box%3Anmah_740693)
- **Rail engagement and flush fasteners.** Compare the rail and block mounting figures. Look at how the rails align to their mounting surfaces, how the table sits on its blocks, and how bolt-hole caps sit flush with the rail. Check the complete swept path separately on the game model. [THK, *LM Guide Mounting Procedure*](https://www.thk.com/us/en/products/lm_guide/maintenance/0002/)
